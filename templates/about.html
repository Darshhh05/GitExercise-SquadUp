Our Purpose

SquadUp makes sports participation easier by connecting students, facilities and campus events in one organized system.

🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage students to stay active by making facility booking, event discovery and team participation faster, clearer and more accessible.

⚡

Why SquadUp?

Finding a court or a team should not be complicated. SquadUp provides a structured system where students can book, join and organize sports activities with ease.

Core Features

The platform is built with practical features that support both students and administrators.

📅

Facility Booking

Students can request bookings and track approval status.

🤝

Join Events

Users can join approved events based on facility, date and level.

🏅

Badges

Players earn achievement badges based on their activity.

🔐

Admin Control

Admins can manage users, bookings, events and announcements.

How It Works

SquadUp follows a simple flow from registration to booking and participation.

1

Create Account

Students register with details, skill level and avatar.

2

Book or Create

Users request a facility booking or create a sports event.

3

Get Approved

Admin reviews bookings and events before users can view them.
